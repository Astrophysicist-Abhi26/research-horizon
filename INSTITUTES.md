# Indian research institutes: coverage list

The leading Indian institutes in the fields Event Horizon 2.0 covers (physics, astronomy,
mathematics, AI/ML and computer science), and whether their events are read today. Use it
to decide which source to add next; each one is checked against the live site before it
goes into [`scraper/institutes.yaml`](scraper/institutes.yaml).

**Status:** ✅ covered (at least one department) · ⏸ tried, can't be read yet (reason given)
· ⬜ not yet. Last checked from GitHub's network on 2026-10-25.

**Priority** (a suggestion): **A** strong research and regular seminars, add first · **B**
next · **C** later or few public events

**Scope.** IITs, IISERs, NISER and the national research institutes (DAE, DST, Department of
Space, and the Indian Statistical Institute) only; no universities. Pure sciences come first: physics, astronomy, mathematics and
statistics. Engineering departments (computer science, electrical engineering) are added
only at the most established IITs (Bombay, Madras, Delhi, Kanpur, Kharagpur), and IIITs only
where a field would otherwise be missing.

**What we collect** is anything a researcher can attend or register for, in person or
online: conferences, workshops, schools, lecture series, colloquia and seminars.

Some events from institutes marked ⬜ already appear indirectly, through INSPIRE (HEP,
gravitation and astrophysics conferences), researchseminars.org and the ASI announcements.
That is no substitute for the institute's own seminar list.

For an IIT or IISER, "the institute" means its relevant departments (Physics, Mathematics,
Earth & Space Sciences; Computer Science and Electrical Engineering at the IITs named above),
each usually with its own seminar page.
